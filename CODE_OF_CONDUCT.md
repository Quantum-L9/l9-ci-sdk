# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Quantum-L9 community harassment-free and
to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints,
constructive feedback, accountability, and focus on the overall community.
Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized conduct, trolling, personal attacks,
harassment, publishing private information without permission, and conduct that
is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The Quantum-L9 platform team (`@Quantum-L9/platform`) is responsible for
clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in community spaces and when an individual officially
represents the community in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through GitHub Security Advisories or directly to
`@Quantum-L9/platform`. Complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. Correction: private warning, clarity, and requested remediation.
2. Warning: consequences for continued behavior and restricted interaction.
3. Temporary ban: temporary removal from community interaction.
4. Permanent ban: removal for sustained, severe, or repeated violations.
